Terms of use

Draft for a pilot. These terms have not been reviewed by a lawyer. The person running this pilot must have them reviewed, especially the parts about responsibility and liability, before inviting real users.

1. What the app is

Be a Guardian is an early pilot. It lets a woman send an SOS to nearby approved volunteers and to her emergency contacts. It supports the police and does not replace them. In an emergency, call 112 first or at the same time.

2. No guarantee

  • We cannot promise that anyone will be nearby, that a helper will respond, or how fast. Phones, networks, location services and notifications can fail.
  • The web version can only alert helpers whose app is open or recently open, and cannot take over a locked phone's screen.
  • Do not rely on the app as your only way to get help.

3. For women using the safety button

  • You agree that your live location is shared with alerted helpers and your emergency contacts while an alert is active.
  • Only press SOS when you need help. Repeated false alarms can lead to your account being suspended.
  • Keep your PIN private. You need it to end an alert.

4. For volunteer helpers

  • Helpers are volunteers, not police, security staff or employees of this service. We check applicants in the ways described to them, but we cannot guarantee that any helper is safe or trustworthy.
  • Call 112 first or at the same time. Do not confront anyone alone. Go to be seen, to stay with her and to bring others. Stay in public places and do not enter private spaces alone.
  • Never contact, follow or share information about anyone you meet through alerts.
  • You agree to a verification check, which may include a police verification certificate and a call from an administrator. Helpers whose checks lapse or fail may be removed.
  • Misuse (stalking, harassment, fake responses, false information) means a permanent ban and, where the law allows, a report to the police.
  • You take part at your own risk. Do not put yourself in danger.
  • No compensation. This app does not pay for, and does not arrange insurance or any other cover for, injury, death, loss or legal costs you may suffer while acting as a guardian. Being a guardian is voluntary and unpaid.
  • You agree to give a photo of an ID and a selfie for the verification check, and you understand how they are stored and deleted as described in the privacy policy.
  • The record of alerts (who was alerted, who responded and when) may be shared with the police if they ask for it.

5. Reports and removal

Anyone can report a helper or a user. Administrators may suspend or remove any account at any time to protect users.
