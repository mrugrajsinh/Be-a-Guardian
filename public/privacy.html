Privacy policy

Draft for a pilot. This text was written as a starting point and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. The person running this pilot must have it reviewed (India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies) and fill in the contact details below before inviting real users.

Who we are

Be a Guardian ("we", "the app") is a pilot safety service. When a woman presses the SOS button, approved volunteer helpers who are on duty near her are alerted with her location. The app supports the police and does not replace them. In an emergency, always call 112.

Contact for questions, corrections and complaints: [add your name and email or phone here].

What we collect

  • Everyone: name, phone number, a scrambled (hashed) version of your PIN, the time you registered and accepted these terms.
  • Women (safety button users): emergency contacts you add (name and phone), and your location, but only while an alert is active.
  • Helpers: the type of ID you chose and only its last 4 digits (never the full number), your note about why you want to help, your duty status, and your location while you are on duty. Helper locations are kept in the server's memory only and are not saved to disk.
  • Alerts: when an alert happened, how it ended, which helpers were alerted and how they responded. The location is erased when the alert ends.
  • Device data: a push-notification address for your phone if you switch on lock-screen alerts.

Why we use it

  • To send an SOS to helpers and contacts, and to show helpers where to go.
  • To decide who may be a helper (verification) and to remove people who misuse the service.
  • To keep the service safe, investigate reports and prevent false alarms.

Who can see your data

  • During an alert: helpers who are alerted see her first name and live location. They do not see her phone number. Her emergency contacts get a message with a map link.
  • Administrators of this pilot can see names, phone numbers, helper ID type and last 4 digits, and alert records.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. We may share information with the police if the law requires it or to help in an emergency.
  • Our hosting provider (Render) and, if switched on, an SMS provider process data on our behalf to run the service.

How long we keep it

  • Location: deleted when an alert ends. Helper locations are never saved to disk.
  • Alert records (without location): kept for up to 30 days.
  • Account details: kept until you ask us to delete your account.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, or delete your account. Contact us at the address above and we will respond. Helpers can switch off duty at any time. You can turn off location permission in your phone settings, but alerts will not work without it.

Security

PINs are stored in hashed form. Connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a PIN that is hard to guess and do not share it.

Who can use the app

The app is for adults (18 and over).

Changes

We may update this policy during the pilot. We will change the date below and tell users in the app where possible.
